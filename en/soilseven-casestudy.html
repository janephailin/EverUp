The situation

A shop with potential — but no consistent growth engine

S-Oil Seven Thailand had been running their TikTok Shop since mid-2025. While the shop saw occasional spikes — notably in October and November 2025 — performance was inconsistent and began declining through December 2025 and January 2026. There was no scalable content or live strategy in place, and the shop was heavily dependent on organic traffic with no affiliate or creator activation framework.

The strategy

Four pillars introduced in February 2026

EverUp's engagement centred on four focused initiatives — building the operational discipline and content infrastructure the shop had been missing.

01
Content diligence

Set clear content output goals to ensure the shop published consistently — giving the algorithm steady signals and customers a reason to return.

02
Live room efficiency

Optimised live session structure, cadence, and in-room tactics to maximise conversion during each broadcast — turning airtime directly into GMV.

03
Campaign planning

Built a structured promotional calendar aligned to platform moments and product launches, ensuring each campaign was set up to convert before it ran.

04
KOL & affiliate leverage

Activated KOLs and affiliate creators to extend reach and generate authentic product content — amplifying the shop's existing marketing momentum at scale.
